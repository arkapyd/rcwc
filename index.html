Predictive Odds Explained

    
// how the predictive engine works

    
    

      
strength: head-to-head 45% (90% manager rivalry · 10% nation rivalry, all banked seasons + live S1) · present form 55% (BASRA, BEWM & current win/loss-streak confidence). the two nations' strengths are then compared head-to-head.

      
the bar: hover any fixture in Up Next. The two strengths are normalised against each other and drawn as a single 100% line — each nation's share painted in its own primary colour. A 50/50 split means the engine can't separate them.

      
reading it: this is a projection from results so far, not a market. Early in the season, with little form banked, most fixtures will sit close to even.

      
predictive & entertainment only.
